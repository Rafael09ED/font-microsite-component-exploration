## hero

- **giant-wordmark** (8): The family name set as large as the viewport allows on a flat field, little else; may mix styles letter by letter to show range. _[merged: hero/style-medley-wordmark]_ — e.g. cnap-fr-sites-infini-en#00, excelsiorama-com#00, karrik-phantom-foundry-com#00
- **kinetic-type** (8): The type in motion is the hero: animated, 3D, generative, video clips, or axes driven by scroll. _[merged: hero/video-mosaic, hero/scroll-driven-axes]_ — e.g. gt-cinetype-com#00, leon-kim-com#00, lozanotype-github-io-java-sans#00
- **sentence-as-specimen** (7): A headline sentence about the font, set in the font (sometimes beside one oversized glyph or rotating through styles): the pitch is the specimen. _[merged: hero/pitch-beside-glyph, hero/mixed-style-pangram]_ — e.g. compressa-preusstype-com#00, faction-losttype-com#00, infinite-tragedy-com#00
- **interactive-specimen** (5): The hero is a specimen the visitor manipulates straight away (axes, weight, contrast, text), via controls or the wheel. — e.g. garet-typeforward-com#00, heydon-works-skwar#00, kachibuwa-com#00
- **typeset-page** (4): A classical typeset composition (title page, broadside, dictionary entry) that uses the family's own hierarchy, italics and ornaments. _[merged: hero/dictionary-entry, hero/title-page]_ — e.g. prospectus-losttype-com#00, vollkorn-typeface-com#00, djr-com-fern#00
- **name-over-photo** (4): Name and a short line over a full-bleed mood photograph. — e.g. aspen-ludwigtype-de#00, gidole-github-io#00, pp-frama-com#00
- **product-landing** (4): The software-product landing pattern: headline, tagline, paragraph, primary and secondary buttons. — e.g. font-subf-dev-en#00, overpassfont-org#00, rubjo-github-io-victor-mono#00
- **construction-drawing** (2): A glyph shown as its construction (Bézier points, skeleton, strokes), often drawn on. — e.g. kermit-font-com#00, djr-com-gimlet-xray#00
- **material-photo** (2): A photograph of physical material (paper, textile, objects) carrying or framing the type. — e.g. colab-munken-com-munken-sans#00, knitgrotesk-com#00
- **illustration-scene** (2): An illustrated scene or mascot staged with the type; the illustration sets the tone. — e.g. gt-maru-com#00, halyard-dardenstudio-com#02
- **prop-collage** (2): A collage of designed artefacts (documents, tickets, packaging, diagrams) set in the font, implying a fictional world. — e.g. departuremono-com#00, gt-mechanik-com#00
- **mini-game** (0): The hero is a small playable game built from the page's own elements (e.g. Pong); collisions or score change the name's axes, CTAs can be game pieces.
- **click-to-stamp** (0): An almost empty first screen; each click drops a word or phrase in the font at the pointer with randomised style or axis values, so the visitor composes the hero.
- **strip-mixer** (0): A typeset page cut into headline, deck and body strips, each its own carousel of styles cycling on its own timer and swipeable, so the page keeps recombining.
- **glyph-grid-sampler** (0): A small name over a large grid of characters from every covered script, often on writing-practice guide cells.

## design-details

- **feature-before-after** (13): A feature or alternate shown off vs on (side by side, overlaid, toggled or typed in a loop) with a short explanation. _[merged: design-details/overlay-comparison, design-details/typewriter-word-cycle]_ — e.g. compressa-preusstype-com#01, djr-com-forma#06, font-subf-dev-en#02
- **axis-explainer** (11): Each variable axis demonstrated on its own: a word plus slider, labelled steps, or animation fitting frames. _[merged: design-details/fit-to-frame-animation, design-details/axis-steps]_ — e.g. almnt-github-io-crimsonpro-microsite#02, gt-flexa-com#02, monolisa-dev#08
- **feature-index** (4): Every stylistic set or ligature group catalogued as cards or tiles (input → output), often toggleable or exportable as settings. _[merged: design-details/feature-index-cards, design-details/feature-config-builder, design-details/alternate-tiles]_ — e.g. vollkorn-typeface-com#01, monolisa-dev#17, exat-hottype-co#07
- **annotated-specimen** (4): Letterforms with technical annotations or numbered figures (measurements, ink traps, angles), one decision per row. _[merged: design-details/numbered-figures]_ — e.g. gt-zirkon-com#03, gt-planar-com#07, betaniapatmos-huertatipografica-com#14
- **system-diagrams** (2): The family's logic (weight curve, optical sizes, widths) explained with charts, like a design-system manual. — e.g. gt-standard-com#03, gt-standard-com#05
- **design-notes** (2): A sequence of design decisions, each with a visual and a short caption, as a carousel or video tiles. _[merged: design-details/design-notes-carousel, design-details/topic-video-tiles]_ — e.g. pacaembu-naipefoundry-com#03, rt-dromo-com#58
- **highlighted-in-context** (0): Ligatures, alternates or special glyphs set inside a display sentence and picked out in an accent colour, discovered in context rather than in a catalogue.
- **hover-css-inspector** (0): A page-wide inspector: hovering any sample shows a crosshair and a tooltip with that text's CSS (size, leading, weight, features).

## specimen-showcase

- **word-stack** (7): Display words or phrases stacked vertically, often cropped, for rhythm and character. — e.g. 2021-typemedia-org-gajana#06, bigshoulders-me#14, gt-ultra-com#07
- **in-use-mockups** (7): Realistic applications (posters, books, screens, signage, client work) as a wall, carousel, shuffler or filmed object. _[merged: specimen-showcase/mockup-wall, specimen-showcase/shuffle-mockup, specimen-showcase/in-use-carousel, specimen-showcase/printed-object-film]_ — e.g. input-djr-com#01, pp-fragment-com#06, halyard-dardenstudio-com#05
- **prop-collage** (4): Invented artefacts (UI windows, tickets, files, stickers) set in the font, collaged into a scene. — e.g. daifoldes-com-eubiescript-sub#01, etunni-github-io-arapey-minisite#05, gt-flaire-com#04
- **poster-series** (4): A sequence of self-contained poster or specimen-page compositions, often pastiching a period. _[merged: specimen-showcase/period-specimen-pages]_ — e.g. cjtype-com-dunbar#01, pleasure-typefaces-pizza#08, arteria-typofonderie-com#06
- **kinetic-scenes** (4): Animated, generative or physics compositions (falling or scattered glyphs, liquid) after the hero. _[merged: specimen-showcase/glyph-scatter]_ — e.g. 2021-typemedia-org-gajana#07, basco-typofonderie-com#03, leon-kim-com#06
- **editorial-in-use** (3): The font running a real or realistic publication (articles, magazine pages) with genuine content. — e.g. etna-marksimonson-com#01, foodissue-commercialtype-com#05, foodissue-commercialtype-com#06
- **use-case-samples** (1): Tabs or panels with realistic content samples per use case (code, data tables, UI). _[merged: specimen-showcase/use-case-tabs]_ — e.g. usgraphics-com-products-berkeley-mono#02
- **modular-grid** (1): A uniform grid of cells mixing large glyphs, sentences and notes, like a specimen sheet. — e.g. compagnon-eesab-fr#03
- **themed-infographic** (1): An interactive infographic on the font's theme, set in the font so the numerals and labels do real work. — e.g. pacaembu-naipefoundry-com#11
- **generative-layout-stream** (0): An endless scroll appending randomly composed layout modules (columns, captions, lists, display words) with themed, often editable copy.
- **slider-morph-mockups** (0): Realistic layouts (ads, tickets, flyers) each with an axis slider that morphs the whole design between the axis extremes.
- **size-waterfall** (0): The same running text at a descending series of labelled sizes, from display to caption, often in several weights side by side; sometimes a light/dark switch.

## scroll-scrubbed-sequence

A pinned, scroll-scrubbed animation (pre-rendered frame sequence or illustration, e.g. doors opening, an object turning or releasing letters) between sections, with little or no text.

**Recognise by:** sticky stage, image changes with scroll

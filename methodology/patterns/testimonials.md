**Recognise by:** huge adoption numbers with captions

| example | look | feel | ux | learn |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [brailleinstitute-org-freefont#03](../../archive/brailleinstitute-org-freefont/blocks/03.jpg) | 'A Font Created for Everyone': '2025 marks 6 years…', then a 2×2 grid of huge figures '153K+' (downloads), '10K+' (websites), '40M+' (impressions via Google Fonts), 'Free' (to download and use. Always.) with ruled captions, and a 'Download Now' button | Proud, momentum | static; button scrolls back to the form | Reuse the big-number grid for adoption metrics: the same component that described the family now proves its reach |

## press-link-list

A dated list of press coverage, one ruled row per article ('date · outlet : headline' with an arrow), each linking to the article; the coverage itself is the social proof, with no quotes or logos.

**Recognise by:** dated rows of press links

![testimonials/press-link-list examples](../variants/testimonials--press-link-list.jpg)

| example | look | feel | ux | learn |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [brailleneue-com#06](../../archive/brailleneue-com/blocks/06.jpg) | 'Media': a hairline-ruled list of ~10 rows, each a date (2018.03.26 …), the outlet and headline in English or Japanese (designboom, CAREER HACK, Spoon & Tamago, Wired Audi Innovation Award, Nikkei, GQ Journal, Design Indaba…) and a → at the right | Orderly, quietly proud | Each row links out to the article in a new tab | A chronological press list shows how the project spread over years and across languages without needing quotes |

An open comment form at the end of the page where visitors post thanks, bug reports and feature requests in public, often with a tally of the most requested features; visitors' voices rather than curated quotes

**Recognise by:** public comment form, requested-features tally

| example | look | feel | ux | learn |
|---|---|---|---|---|
